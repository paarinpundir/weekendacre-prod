Not organic. Not “natural.” Produce from farms we’ve visited, monitored, and continue to know.

We walk the fields. We ask about water sources, soil treatment, and what inputs are used. We eat a meal there. We meet the family. We share what we find — including when things change.

01 — Seasonal, and honest about it

We don’t carry 250 items.

We carry what’s growing right now, from farms that currently have it. Seasonal means the food is at its best — not the widest shelf.

What you’ll never see us do: source from the wholesale market and re-label it as farm produce. We know the difference. So will you.

The tomato isn’t just a tomato anymore — it’s Bijendra Kumar’s tomato, from the field you walked through.

When you’ve spent a weekend on a farm and then order their produce the following week, something changes in how you relate to your food. That connection is what we’re building, one fortnight at a time.
